# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We are a small group of people trying to make mind maps carry real knowledge instead of decoration.
Participants agree to:

- Argue about the artifact, not the person. A standard is right or wrong on evidence, not on rank.
- Assume a reported defect means a real gap in a gate, and say plainly what was verified and how.
- Keep the discussion about the capability: standards, gates, tooling, knowledge documents.
- Respect that a rule may look excessive and still be there because of a specific failure nobody
  outside the room saw. Ask, then propose.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Publishing or requesting material that discloses someone's private paths, account names,
  credentials or unpublished documents — see [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
- Dismissing a claim by asserting a result without the command that produced it.
- Harassment, personal attacks, and gatekeeping by tone.
- Silently softening a standard inside someone else's deliverable instead of proposing the change.

## Enforcement

Report conduct issues the same way as security issues: privately, via GitHub's private reporting
channels for this repository, never by naming the offending content in a public thread. Maintainers
respond within 14 days; repeated or egregious behaviour leads to being reported to GitHub.

## Attribution

This is a short, project-specific code of conduct for a documentation repository. It replaces no
platform rules; GitHub's Community Guidelines also apply.
